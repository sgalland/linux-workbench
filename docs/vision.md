# Vision

Linux Workbench defines a preferred Linux workstation and a safe method for describing, inspecting, planning, and eventually managing its configuration. It is independent of any particular distribution, desktop environment, AI agent, or model provider.

The project aims to make workstation intent understandable to people and agents, keep machine-specific needs distinct from shared preferences, and turn proven solutions into deterministic, reviewable configuration. Agents should help with novel problems; known solutions should be encoded so they do not require repeated AI reasoning.

CachyOS is the initial distribution target. KDE Plasma is the first desktop environment to investigate, as an implementation adapter rather than part of the abstract specification. The first machine profile will eventually cover an HP Envy laptop. Its problematic Bang & Olufsen internal-speaker configuration is known but out of scope for diagnosis or changes at this stage.
